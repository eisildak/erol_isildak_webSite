Privacy Policy

Beacon Broadcaster App

Data Collection

Beacon Broadcaster does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app operates entirely on your device.

Bluetooth Usage

This app uses Bluetooth technology to broadcast iBeacon signals. The beacon UUID, major, minor, and other parameters are configured locally on your device and are not shared with any external servers.

Location Services

Location services may be required by iOS for Bluetooth beacon broadcasting. However, no location data is collected, stored, or transmitted by this application.

Third-Party Services

This app does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Data Storage

All beacon configuration settings are stored locally on your device using UserDefaults. This data never leaves your device.

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
